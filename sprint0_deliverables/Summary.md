# Project Summary

## Part 1: Project Objective
The objective of this project is to develop a centralized platform for York University events, combining information from student clubs and online communities such as Discord servers and Instagram profiles.  

The app will automatically collect, organize, and display event data through web scraping and possible APIs, providing students with a unified and accessible event calendar.  

Our goal is to increase student engagement and participation by making it easier to discover relevant academic, social, and club-related events.  

---

## Part 2: Key Usage Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Event Discovery and Filtering
- An active student opens the app, selects filters like **“Career Development”** and **“Campus Life”**, or searches for key events (e.g., *Smash Bros Tourney*).  
- The app immediately displays a detailed list of upcoming events from both official York sources and student clubs.

### Scenario 2: Calendar-Based View
- Users can view all activities in a **calendar by day**, making it easy to see what’s happening without manually searching.  
- This view supports casual browsing: students can discover interesting events day by day.

### Scenario 3: Event Promotion for Clubs
- A club organizer submits a new event through a simple form or by connecting their Discord server.  
- The event is automatically published on the main app feed, ensuring visibility across the entire student body without requiring extra posting effort.

---

## Part 3: Key Users

### User Style 1: Students
- **Event finders:** Average YorkU students looking to get in touch with the community but who don’t have the time to research events manually.

### Principle 2: Club Executives
- Users who run clubs and want to expand their reach.  
- They can add our Discord bot, allow website scraping, or manually add events to ensure visibility.

### Principle 3: Admin-Level Oversight (e.g., YFS)
- Responsible for monitoring on-campus activities.  
- Should be able to conveniently view most events for administrative purposes.

---

## Part 4: Key Principles

### Principle 1: Accessibility and Ease of Use
- Prioritize a clean, simple interface that allows users to **find or post events quickly**, without technical knowledge or multiple logins.

### Principle 2: Inclusivity and Fair Visibility
- All student organizations, regardless of size or recognition level, should have **equal visibility** in the event feed to ensure a diverse and inclusive experience.

### Principle 3: Automation and Efficiency
- Minimize manual effort through **automated scraping, integration, and updates**, keeping the platform accurate and current.

### Principle 4: Ethical Scraping and TOS Compliance
- Ensure that any scraping tools or bots are permitted by **club executives** and **York University** through explicit permission.
