# [YUConnect](https://yuconnect.yorku.ca/home_login)

**YUConnect** is York University’s official online platform for getting involved and engaging with student life at York. It provides many features that encompass, but are not limited to, the social aspect of being a York University student. One of these features is the platform for **student organizations to post their upcoming events**.

This platform seems to solve the problem of students being unable to easily discover student-led events, especially those that the student is not even aware of. However, a major flaw from a user perspective is that **there are currently not many events listed**. All the events that are listed on YUConnect are from the same handful of student organizations. This suggests difficulties in using the platform from the perspective of an executive listing an event. As a result, the platform is unable to fully resolve the initial problem.

Our product seeks to solve this problem and avoid the issues that YUConnect faces by **automating the process of posting events**. Through automatic web scraping, we can aggregate events hosted by organizations onto a single platform dedicated to **student-driven occasions**. By reducing the effort an executive must make to the bare minimum, our platform will be **simpler and easier to use** compared to the manual posting of events in YUConnect.

A student considering using our application or YUConnect requires a **diverse and comprehensive list of events** held by clubs and organizations they may or may not already be connected with. It is important for a student to be able to find events that they do not know exist. While most clubs use **Discord** and/or **Instagram** to distribute information, these platforms are typically unable to present club events that the user is not already following. Our platform addresses this by allowing students to **discover new events and broaden their interests** in a single, dedicated location.

YUConnect was likely built to be the encompassing web application for all things social at York University. Most importantly, it is **operating in an official capacity** as a York University-owned product. While it may not be difficult in theory for them to implement auto scrapers and other tools to increase club engagement with their event discovery, **it would not be appropriate for them to do so**.

**Instagram** and **Discord** are major social media platforms that most clubs use. However, they are not built for the expressed purpose of increasing club visibility. Their popularity is due to convenience — students can easily follow any club they want as existing users. Our product provides an edge over Instagram and Discord by **compartmentalizing this service into a separate platform**, reducing clutter in students’ social media feeds, which may be desirable for some.
